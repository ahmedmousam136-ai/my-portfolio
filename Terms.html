The Facility’s owner sets up the account and decides which staff members get access. Staff sign in with different roles, such as owner, receptionist and doctor, and each role sees only the screens meant for it.

3Plan, price and allowances

The AuraScale OS plan is ₹4,999 per month per Facility. Prices are in Indian Rupees. GST and other taxes are added where they apply.

  • Each month includes 300 ID-card scans. This monthly allowance resets each billing month and does not carry over.
  • If you need more, you can buy a top-up of ₹499, which adds 100 ID-card scans. Top-up credits do not expire.
  • When the scans run out, ID scanning stops until the next month or until you top up. You can always type information in by hand.

4Payments

Payments are processed securely by Cashfree Payments. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only the payment status and reference numbers we need to activate your plan.

Fees are charged in advance for each month. Please see our Refund & Cancellation Policy for cancellations and refunds.

5Late or missed payments

If a monthly payment is not made by its due date, you get a 7-day grace period to pay. After that, the Facility’s account is frozen: the owner and reception screens are locked until payment is made.

So that patient care is not interrupted, the doctor screens stay available while an account is frozen. Your data is not deleted when an account is frozen, and full access returns once the payment is received.

7AI-assisted ID scanning

One feature uses artificial intelligence: reading text from an ID-card photo to pre-fill a registration form. It is there to save typing, and it can make mistakes.

Always check before you save. Staff must check the details filled in by the scan. The Facility stays responsible for the accuracy of patient records and bills.

When a bill is calculated, the total is always worked out by the software as quantity × unit price. It is never decided by AI. If a quantity or a price is missing, the software asks for it and will not save an incomplete charge.
