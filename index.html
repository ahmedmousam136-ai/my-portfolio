How It Works

The Complete Facility Workflow. In Eight Steps.

AuraScale OS connects every stage of the patient journey — from the first visit to the final discharge — in one platform.

01
Register Patient
Capture patient identity, medical history and contacts with AI-assisted document extraction.
02
Create Admission
Open an admission record with diagnosis, attending doctor and expected timeline.
03
Assign Bed & Doctor
Allocate the right bed type and assign a doctor from your panel based on availability.
04
Manage Treatment & Documents
Doctors update notes, prescriptions and reports. Documents are stored against the patient's record.
05
Generate Bill
Create an itemised bill covering room, doctor, procedures, medicines and all applicable charges.
06
Explore Coverage / Payment Route
Review applicable government scheme, insurance or self-pay options for the patient.
07
Complete Payment / Settlement
Process payment through the selected route and mark the billing record accordingly.
08
Discharge & Maintain Record
Issue discharge summary, update bed status and preserve the complete digital record.
