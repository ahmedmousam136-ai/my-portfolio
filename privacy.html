1Who this policy covers

This policy explains what information AuraScale OS (“AuraScale”, “we”), a sole proprietorship of Mousham Ahmed Laskar (GSTIN 19BDYPL9210K1ZR), collects, why, and how it is protected. It covers nursing homes and clinics that use AuraScale OS (each a “Facility”), their staff, their patients, and visitors to aurascale.in.

Two roles under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For patient information, the Facility is the Data Fiduciary: it decides what is recorded and why, and AuraScale is its Data Processor, acting only on its instructions. For Facility staff accounts and website enquiries, AuraScale is the Data Fiduciary. Patients can read our short notice for patients.

2What we collect

  • Account details of Facility staff: name, email, phone number, role and the Facility’s name, address, state and GSTIN.
  • Patient and admission records that Facility staff enter: name, phone number, age or date of birth, gender, address, guardian, bed and admission details, doctor, diagnosis notes, charges, stock used, payments and bills, and uploaded documents such as ID-card photos, bills and reports.
  • WhatsApp permission: whether the patient tapped “Yes” or “No” on the permission message, and the messages sent.
  • Subscription payments made to AuraScale by UPI (and, where offered, through Cashfree Payments): amount, date and payment reference. We never see your UPI PIN, card or bank login.
  • Agreement acceptance: the owner’s name, date, time and version when the Service Agreement is accepted in the portal.
  • Technical records needed to keep the service secure, such as sign-in activity and an audit trail of important actions.
  • Website enquiries: the demo request form on aurascale.in opens your own email app with the details you typed, and they reach us only if you press send. The website does not save form entries on a server.

3ID cards and government ID numbers

When staff photograph a patient’s ID card, the image is read by AI to pre-fill name, date of birth, gender, address and, where printed, a phone number. AuraScale does not read or store Aadhaar numbers from the photo.

Staff may type an ID number into an optional field. We advise Facilities not to record Aadhaar numbers unless they are legally allowed to, and each Facility is responsible for following the rules that apply to it.

4How we use information

  • to provide, secure and support AuraScale OS for the Facility: registration, beds, stock, billing, payments and reports;
  • to send WhatsApp messages to a patient only after the patient taps “Yes” on the permission message, and to stop when they tap “No”;
  • to send the Facility owner daily and monthly reports;
  • to manage subscriptions, top-ups and our GST invoices;
  • to answer questions and fix problems;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, and do not build profiles of patients.

5Outside providers that help us

We use a small number of trusted providers (“sub-processors”). Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase (Singapore): our database, sign-in system and private file storage.
  • Make (European Union): automations that send WhatsApp messages and reports.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business: delivering WhatsApp messages to patients and owners.
  • Cashfree Payments (India): online payments, where offered.
  • Anthropic (USA): reading text from ID-card photos.

Patient records are stored in Singapore. The DPDP Act allows this unless the Government of India restricts transfers to that country. We tell Facilities at least 15 days before adding or changing a sub-processor.

6ID photos and scans

A photo used for an ID scan is sent to the provider above, which returns the text to the screen. The scanning step itself does not keep the image. If staff choose to save the ID photo, or upload another document such as a bill, it is stored in the Facility’s private file storage.

7Who can see information

Information is separated by Facility, and inside a Facility each staff member sees only what their role allows. We do not share personal information with anyone else except the providers above or when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid legal order.

8Security and data breaches

We protect information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted storage, separate access for each Facility and role, secret keys kept only on our servers, two-step login on our own accounts, backups, and an audit trail of important actions kept for at least one year. We also ask Facilities to keep passwords private and to remove access for staff who leave.

If a data breach happens, we tell the affected Facility within 24 hours and help it inform the affected patients without delay and the Data Protection Board of India (with a detailed report within 72 hours), as the DPDP Rules require. Cyber security incidents are reported to CERT-In within 6 hours.

9How long we keep information

  • We keep information while the Facility’s account is active. When an account is paused for non-payment, its data is kept and not deleted.
  • When a Facility leaves, we give it a full export of its data on request within 30 days, and delete its data from AuraScale 60 days after the service ends; backups are overwritten within a further 30 days. The Facility keeps its own medical and billing records for as long as the law requires of it.
  • We keep our own billing and tax records for as long as GST law requires, and security logs for at least one year.

10Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to see a summary of the information held about you, to correct or update it, to have it erased when it is no longer needed, to withdraw consent (for example, to stop WhatsApp messages), to name someone to act for you, and to complain about how it is handled.

For patient records, please start with the Facility you visited, because it holds and controls your record. If you write to us, we pass your request to the Facility within 2 working days and help it reply. We and the Facility aim to reply within 7 days, and always within the 90 days the DPDP Rules allow.

11Children

Patient records can include children. For a patient under 18, or a person who cannot consent, the Facility records the consent of a parent or lawful guardian. AuraScale does not collect information directly from children.

12Cookies and website tracking

The AuraScale website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows an embedded Google Map, which are served by Google and may receive your IP address and browser details. The AuraScale OS software keeps you signed in using your browser’s storage.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. We tell Facility owners by email at least 15 days before a change that affects how patient information is handled.
